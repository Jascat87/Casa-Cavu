Oceanside Serenity Awaits

Casa Cavu features an open-concept floor plan designed to frame sweeping views of the turquoise sea. The bright main level blends a spacious living lounge with a fully equipped chef's kitchen and breakfast bar, opening directly onto a private beachfront swimming pool via floor-to-ceiling glass sliding doors.

Enjoy your mornings paddleboarding across the calm bay, walk to neighboring oceanfront restaurants, or watch the stars from the massive panoramic rooftop observation deck.

Location: Tankah Tres (Tankah Bay), Quintana Roo, Mexico
